# Corporate sales SLA

## Response times
- Hot lead — 15 minutes
- Qualified lead — 2 hours during business hours
- Proposal delivery — 24 hours

## Renewal workflow
- 45 days before expiry: schedule review meeting
- 30 days before expiry: send renewal proposal
- 14 days before expiry: reminder call
- 7 days before expiry: escalation notice

## Ownership
Each account has one owner.
No dual owners.
