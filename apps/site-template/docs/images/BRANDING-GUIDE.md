# BRANDING GUIDE — Belleza Studio

## 1. Brand Identity

### Core Identity
- **Business Name:** Belleza Studio
- **Tagline:** "Tu belleza, nuestro compromiso"
- **Location:** Asunción, Paraguay
- **Type:** Premium beauty salon (hair, nails, eyebrows, bridal)
- **Positioning:** High-quality professional beauty services at accessible prices in Asunción's premium segment

### Brand Personality
- **Warm** — Every client feels welcomed and cared for
- **Professional** — Certified experts using premium products
- **Transformative** — We don't just do hair, we transform confidence
- **Honest** — No hidden costs, clear pricing, real results

### Voice & Tone
- Warm, personal, approachable — like talking to a trusted friend who happens to be a beauty expert
- Professional but not stiff — we know our craft deeply but don't talk down
- Confident without being arrogant — we know we deliver great results
- Local Paraguayan identity — warm Latin American warmth with Asunción urban sophistication

## 4. Visual Style & Aesthetic

### Overall Aesthetic
**"Warm Editorial Luxury"** — Clean, spacious layouts with warm tones. Think high-end women's magazine beauty editorial but approachable. Not stark or cold — warm, inviting, aspirational but achievable.

### Key Visual Principles
1. **Warmth over coolness** — Coral and rose accents soften the navy/white base
2. **Spacious layouts** — Generous padding, breathing room, not cluttered
3. **Editorial photography style** — Professional beauty photography, not stock
4. **Transformation focus** — Before/after imagery is central to brand
5. **Real faces, real beauty** — Authentic women, not overly retouched models
6. **Paraguayan warmth** — Latin American beauty, Asunción urban setting

### Imagery Style Guide
- **Photography style:** Professional beauty editorial (like Allure, Vogue Beauty)
- **Lighting:** Warm, flattering, natural-feeling with professional softbox setup
- **Backgrounds:** Clean, neutral, warm (no stark white, no cold grays)
- **Skin tones:** Diverse Paraguayan/Latin American skin tones (medium warm)
- **Hair focus:** Beautiful, healthy-looking hair — shine, movement, color depth
- **Mood:** Confident, warm, aspirational but approachable
- **Framing:** Tight on subject (face/hair focus), clean negative space for text overlay

### NOT to use:
- ❌ Stark white backgrounds (too clinical)
- ❌ Overly edited/retouched models (unrealistic)
- ❌ Cold blue/gray tones
- ❌ Generic stock photos of beauty (must feel local/authentic)
- ❌ Clip art, illustrations, icons over photos for main imagery
- ❌ Corporate/lawyer headshot style portraits

## 5. Brand Elements

### Logo Treatment
- Clean, modern, minimal text-based logo
- "Belleza" in bold, "Studio" in lighter weight
- Works on light and dark backgrounds
- Icon version: stylized "B" or scissors+haircomb motif (future)

### Decorative Elements
- Subtle gradient overlays on hero images (navy to transparent)
- Soft rounded corners (8-16px) on cards
- Rose gold accents for premium sections
- Subtle drop shadows for depth (not heavy)

### Icon Style
- Lucide React Icons (consistent, clean line icons)
- Custom color for each service category (see color palette)
- Icons at 24px standard, 16px inline

### Photography Treatment
- Slight warm color grade (vs. cool/neutral)
- Rich contrast — not flat, not over-processed
- Skin tones: warm, natural, healthy-looking
- Hair: shine, dimension, movement
- Beauty retouching: natural, not heavy

## 7. Competitive Differentiation

### Position vs. Other Asunción Salons
| Factor | Belleza Studio | Typical Asunción Salon |
|--------|---------------|----------------------|
| Products | Wella, Schwarzkopf, Olaplex | Often lower-tier |
| Expertise | Certified colorists, 10+ years | Variable |
| Atmosphere | Premium studio, climatizado | Often basic |
| Pricing | Transparent, all-inclusive | Often hidden costs |
| Guarantee | Satisfaction guarantee | Rarely offered |
| Convenience | WhatsApp booking, parking | Often neither |

### Unique Selling Points
1. **Premium products, accessible prices** — Wella/Schwarzkopf at fair prices
2. **Satisfaction guarantee** — "Si no estás contenta, lo corregimos sin costo"
3. **Full transformation** — Hair + nails + brows + bridal = one-stop beauty
4. **Transparent pricing** — No surprises, all products included
5. **Modern studio** — Air conditioned, wifi, coffee, parking
