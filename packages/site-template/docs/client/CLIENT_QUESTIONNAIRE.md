### 5.2 If You Don't Have Photos

We can:
1. Use professional stock photos of your industry (no extra cost)
2. Schedule a photoshoot (quoted separately)
3. Use placeholder demo images initially

### 5.3 Photo Guidelines

If providing photos:
- Minimum 1MB per image
- JPEG or PNG
- Natural lighting preferred
- Show your real work (not stock)
- Include variety: wide shots, close-ups, products
