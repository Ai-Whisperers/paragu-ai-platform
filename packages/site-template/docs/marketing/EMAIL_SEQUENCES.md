# EMAIL SEQUENCES
## Automated Emails After Client Signup

**Purpose:** Define the automated email sequence that goes out after a new client signs up for the website service. Builds engagement, reduces churn, and drives adoption of key features.

---

## SEQUENCE OVERVIEW

| Email | Day | Purpose | Subject Line |
|-------|-----|--------|-------------|
| 1. Welcome | Day 0 | Introduce, set expectations | "Welcome to ParaguAI Builder — here's what happens next" |
| 2. Getting Started | Day 1 | Send questionnaire, explain | "Let's get your website started — answer these 5 questions" |
| 3. Content Tips | Day 3 | Help them prepare | "How to take photos that make your website shine" |
| 4. Feature Spotlight | Day 5 | Drive feature adoption | "Did you know your website can do this?" |
| 5. Progress Check | Day 7 | Re-engage stalled | "Your website is waiting — here's how to launch" |

## EMAIL TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

### Infrastructure
- Send via: Brevo (Sendinblue), Mailchimp, or Supabase Edge Functions
- Track: Open rate, click rate, unsubscribe rate
- Optimize: Subject line A/B testing

### Timing
- Best send time: Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am (PYT)
- Frequency: Monthly max for newsletters
- Transactional: Immediate (welcome, booking confirmations)

### Compliance
- Include unsubscribe link
- Physical address in footer
- GDPR/PDPA compliant consent
- One-click unsubscribe
