# DEMO CONTENT PLAN — Paraguay Business Website Template

## The Story This Template Tells

**Core Narrative:** "Every day without a website, you're losing customers to competitors who already have one."

**Target Audience:** Paraguayan business owners who:
- Have a business but no professional website
- Run everything through WhatsApp
- Don't know where their customers come from
- Are losing business to competitors who ARE online
- Think "I don't need a website" but are wrong

**The Promise:** A professional website that:
1. Makes you look legitimate/professional
2. Captures customers who search Google
3. Frees you from WhatsApp overload
4. Helps you track and retain customers
5. Grows your business 24/7 even while you sleep

## Content Rewrites Needed

### HERO Section
**Current (beauty-specific):** "Tu belleza, nuestro compromiso"
**New (transformation/why):** "Tu negocio merece estar en internet"

**Slides concept:**
1. "7 de cada 10 negocios en Paraguay NO tienen página web" — Stats visual, urgency
2. "Mientras no tengas web, tu competencia te está robando clientes" — Competitive threat
3. "Hoy es el día — tu negocio merece presencia digital" — Call to action

### REASONS Section
**Rename to "¿Por qué tu negocio necesita una página web?"**

| # | Title | Pain Point | Solution |
|---|-------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | "Tus clientes te buscan en Google" | You're invisible to 70% of potential customers who search online | Website = appear in Google results |
| 2 | "Tu competencia ya tiene web" | Competitors with websites look more serious and capture first contact | Don't lose leads to better-presented competitors |
| 3 | "WhatsApp no es suficiente" | Overwhelmed with messages, can't track history, no booking | Website with booking = organized, professional |
| 4 | "No sabés quién entra a tu negocio" | Running blind, no data on customers | Website analytics reveal who's visiting |
| 5 | "Perdés clientes por no tener horario online" | People call when you're closed, results in lost business | 24/7 online presence with booking |
| 6 | "No tenés cómo mantener clientes" | One-time visitors, no loyalty, no repeat business | Loyalty program + gift cards = customer retention |

### PROCESS Section
**"3 pasos para tener tu página web"**
1. **Elegí tu tipo de negocio** — Templates para cualquier rubro
2. **Completá tus datos** — Servicios, precios, horarios, fotos
3. **¡Listo!** — En minutos tenés una página profesional

### STATS Section
- 7,463 negocios identificados en Paraguay
- 75% no tienen presencia web
- 209 ciudades cubiertas
- Y la cifra crece cada día

### BEFORE/AFTER Section (KEY)
**Rename to "¿Cómo era antes vs. cómo es ahora?"**

| Antes (Sin Web) | Ahora (Con Web) |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| Todo por WhatsApp, mensajes mezclados | Reservas por web, organizadas por fecha |
| Notas en papel o en el celular | Todo digital, historial de clientes |
| Efectivo only, no sabés cuánto vendés | Pagos digitales, reportes claros |
| Competidor aparece en Google, vos no | Aparecés en Google, competís igual |
| Dependés del pasa-palabra | Clientes te encuentran solos |
| Imposible tener clientes nuevos de noche | Página trabaja 24/7 |

## Target Verticals for Demo Content

Since we can't shoot for all verticals, we'll use **"Consultoría de Negocios"** as the demo business type — it's the most generic and relatable:
- Business consulting firm
- Offers advisory services, courses, digital products
- Appeals to other business owners
- Transformation story: informal → professional

**But all the features work for ANY business type:**
- Hair salon? → Booking + loyalty + gift cards
- Restaurant? → Menu + reservations + reviews
- Rock bar? → Events + booking + tickets
- E-commerce? → Products + cart + checkout
- Gym? → Class booking + membership + progress tracking

## Emotional Journey for Visitors

1. **AWARENESS** → "7 de cada 10 negocios no tienen web" (shock value)
2. **RECOGNITION** → "Eso soy yo" (WhatsApp chaos, no data)
3. **FEAR** → "Mi competencia me está robando clientes" (urgency)
4. **HOPE** → "Hay una solución" (features shown)
5. **BELIEF** → "Others like me did it" (testimonials)
6. **ACTION** → "I want this" (CTA)

## Implementation Notes

- All content should be in **Spanish (Paraguayan Spanish)**
- Use **Guaraní touches** where natural ("Jopara" concept — mixed culture)
- Prices in **Guaraníes (Gs.)** — e.g., "Desde Gs. 150.000/mes"
- Include **local payment methods** mention (Bancard, Tigo Money, Mercado Pago)
- Asunción-specific references in imagery
- Testimonials should be **real-sounding Paraguayan Spanish** (not formal Spain Spanish)
