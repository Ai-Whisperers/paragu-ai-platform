## Key Pain Points Identified

### 1. Lost Customers to Competitors
- **Message:** "Su competidor ya tiene web y está robando sus clientes"
- **Proof:** Google Maps data shows competitor websites capturing search traffic
- **Emotional hook:** FOMO + competitive threat

### 2. WhatsApp Overload
- **Message:** "Todo se maneja por WhatsApp, pero WhatsApp no basta"
- **Pain:** Business owner overwhelmed with messages, can't track customer history, no booking system
- **Stats:** Average beauty salon spends 3-4 hours/day on WhatsApp

### 3. No Online Discovery
- **Message:** "Tus clientes te buscan en Google y no te encuentran"
- **Impact:** Being "invisible" to the 70% of Paraguayans who search local businesses online

### 4. Looks Unprofessional
- **Message:** "Sin página web pareces poco serio"
- **Perception:** Competitors with websites are perceived as more established/trustworthy

### 5. No Data, No Insights
- **Message:** "¿Quién entra a tu negocio? No lo sabes"
- **Pain:** Running blind without analytics, customer acquisition sources, peak hours

### 6. Cash Only / No Digital Payments
- **Message:** "Te manejás con papel y lapicera"
- **Impact:** Can't track sales, no loyalty program, no online presence for payments

### 7. Word of Mouth Only
- **Message:** "Dependés del pasa-palabra"
- **Limitation:** Growth capped by personal network reach

## For This Repo: What Image Strategy Serves These Businesses

### The Transformation We Need to Show
For a hair salon owner looking at this template:
- **BEFORE:** Handwritten notes, WhatsApp chaos, no show bookings, cash only, "¿Cuándo fue la última clienta?"
- **AFTER:** Online booking, loyalty points, gift cards sold, customers find you on Google, professional image

For a rock bar owner:
- **BEFORE:** Instagram DMs only, no way to show events, looks amateur
- **AFTER:** Event calendar, ticket sales, reservation system, professional presence

For a restaurant owner:
- **BEFORE:** Paper menus, phone calls only, lost reservations
- **AFTER:** Online ordering, menu with photos, reviews, delivery integration

### Generic/Demo Content Strategy
Since we can't shoot for every business type, the demo content should:
1. **Tell the STORY of transformation** — Not show specific services
2. **Use REAL Paraguayan people** — Authenticity matters
3. **Include LOCAL context** — Asunción landmarks, Guaraní touches, Paraguayan business owners
4. **Show FEATURES working** — Each feature section should show a practical example

## Image Direction Conclusions

### DO:
- ✅ Real Paraguayan entrepreneurs in real work settings
- ✅ Before/after transformations (informal → professional)
- ✅ WhatsApp + notebook/messy desk → Clean dashboard/system
- ✅ Asunción city context (skyline, local businesses, street scenes)
- ✅ Diverse business types represented (not just beauty)
- ✅ People looking at phones/laptops with purpose/growth
- ✅ Social proof moments (testimonials, reviews)
- ✅ Booking system / calendar visuals
- ✅ Local payment methods (Tigo Money, Mercado Pago, Bancard)

### DON'T:
- ❌ Beauty-only imagery (this is a GENERAL business template)
- ❌ Stock photo looking people
- ❌ Foreign models/locations
- ❌ Cold corporate aesthetic
- ❌ Abstract-only (need real human connection)
