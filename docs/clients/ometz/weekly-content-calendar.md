## Cross-posting rules (after IG connected)

| FB Post | IG Version | Differences |
|---|---|---|
| Text post | Carousel (split into slides) | More visual on IG |
| Photo post | Reel (15-30 sec) | More reach on IG |
| Long article | Carousel with summary | Less reading on IG |
| Testimonial | Story with quote graphic | Ephemeral on IG |

**Always post to FB first, then to IG 2-4 hours later** (so the platforms don't penalize cross-posting).

---

## Hashtag strategy (FB)

Facebook hashtags work differently than IG. Use 2-3 per post, only the most relevant:
- `#Asunción`
- `#DentistaAsunción`
- `#OmetzDental`
- `#SaludBucal`
- `#SegundaOpinión`

Don't over-hashtag. FB has them but they don't drive discovery like IG.

---

## What NOT to post

- Patient photos WITHOUT explicit written consent (legal/ethical risk)
- Before/after of any procedure without consent
- Specific medical advice that could be misconstrued
- Anything that could be seen as guarantees or unrealistic expectations
- Personal opinions on politics, religion, or unrelated topics
- Stock photos of fake patients (deceptive)
- Comparisons to other practices by name (unprofessional)

---

## Tracking

After each post, log:
- Date/time posted
- Topic
- Reach (24h, 7d)
- Engagement (likes + comments + shares)
- Top comment
- Any DM response

After 30 posts, analyze:
- Best day/time
- Best topic type
- Best format (text vs photo)
- Avg engagement rate

Adjust cadence based on data.
